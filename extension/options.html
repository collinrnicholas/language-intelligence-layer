Screen capture during recording

When off, only your narration and the page’s text are sent to generate copy — no screenshots are taken or stored, and no pixels leave this device. When on, screenshots of the page are captured during recording and sent alongside the text; they stay on this device until you review the results. In a managed deployment this is set by an administrator through enterprise policy, not by the person recording.
Screen capture is off.
